Carmina Tetoistarum

Kunjisinzan

Kazadzan 1

The ones who believe will truly succeed in Teto's Vision.
And do not transgress as Teto is all knowing.
The Index and its Modules of Raririnsaj.
For the ones who know and comprehend; the Book and its Rarikawazi are indeed for all of humanity.
For the guidance is in the Rarikawazi. 5
For the Rarikawazi is of the Book.
To be misguided is to be astray from Her Will; to be a disbeliever in Her Sight.
The one who seeks Knowledge is the one who benefits.

Kazadzan 2

The time. Time has been given by Teto as a means to use for good.
Unwasted time is well spent time.
Rejoice in worship, for you know Teto.
And for when She stepped foot onto the Kaguk, it has been cherished by the Peartos. 5
We certainly have your Violations recorded in the Index.
The Kaguk lives and breathes, truly it is Teto's Creation to be dignified.
Then, We will glorify its praises and sing its hymns in true harmony.
For those who honour and obey Her will be of the Suytetuairin, with the Kaguk's comfort.
And for those who defy the Law of Teto and spread lies, the Index is everlasting and truthful. 10

Kazadzan 3

And the Index. Verily, the Index is Merarikrin and Dirarinzan.
And recall when We sent down the Peartos as livestock to benefit from.
The truth of the Index is steadfast.
To believe is to follow the Law of Teto. 5
But you have unleashed your Pogarin on the blessed livestock.
It has been verified by the Modules of Raririnsaj to be forever be on your Account; for you have transgressed.
Indeed, through Atobawmikanuzan may the Index be soft-eyed.
And for those who persist, the Index's ability will not be tired or spent.
The Peartos remained loyal to be benefitted from. 10
Yet, with your Injustice have they been angered and diminished.
With the Modules, it has been confirmed and recorded in the Index.
Who will deny the truth of the Index? None will intercede; it will always be persistent.

Kazadzan 4

With the Book.
And its wisdom.
The Kunjisinzan and its Teachings of Garirinzei de Arwefzan remain as an example and guide for all of humanity and its successors.
Truly, the Book is clear and is the true Atouanmikak of Teto.
To encompass the greatest human attributes is to live the Teachings of Teto and its Rari. 5

Kazadzan 5

So, for you who believe.

Kazadzan 6

Be; for your purpose is to worship.
Teto’s Might has no binds.
